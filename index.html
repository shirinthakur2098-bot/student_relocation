Questions you might have

Can't see yours? Ask me in the free consultation. That's what it's for.

When should I get in touch?

As soon as you know your university and roughly when you're arriving. The paid search runs over three weeks, and you'll want time after that to review a contract and agree it with the landlord. In the free consultation we look at your dates together and I'll tell you honestly whether the timing is workable.

Which areas do you cover?

I work within a defined local service area in [Service city]: [describe service area, e.g. districts or travel radius]. If your university or preferred neighbourhood is outside it, mention this when you book. Any work beyond the area is quoted and agreed in advance.

What do the fees include, and what costs extra?

The €350 and €500 fees cover the work described in each package for one student over three weeks. Rent, deposits, agency fees and any other third-party charges are separate and paid directly by you. If you need more than the package includes, such as extra viewings, I'll quote it first and only go ahead once you agree.

Can you guarantee I'll get a room?

No. The fee covers the shortlisting, viewings, reports and (in the €500 package) contract support I deliver. Whether you secure a tenancy depends on what's available and on the landlord's approval.

Is the contract support legal advice?

No. You get an English translation of one rental contract of up to 10 pages, a plain-language summary of the key terms, and a written list of unclear points, concerns and questions to raise with the landlord. It is not a certified translation and not legal advice. For either of those, you would need a certified translator or a qualified lawyer.

What happens if a landlord cancels a viewing?

Landlord-cancelled appointments don't count towards your three completed viewings, so a cancellation doesn't use one of yours up.

Do I need to be available for the viewings?

Ideally, yes. You join each viewing on a live video call so you can see the space and hear the landlord's answers yourself. I coordinate the times with you and the landlord or agent. You also get photos and a written checklist afterwards, so nothing depends on memory.

How quickly will you reply during the search?

Both paid packages include WhatsApp support during the search, with replies within one business day.
